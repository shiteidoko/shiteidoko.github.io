### 💡 Why practice Ju-Jitsu?

* **Balanced physical development:** Improves flexibility, strength, coordination, and endurance.
* **Effective self-defense:** Learn practical techniques for controlling high-pressure situations.
* **Discipline and character:** Builds self-confidence, respect, and strong team spirit.
