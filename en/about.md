### What styles do we practice?

At our club in Constanța, you can learn Ju-Jitsu:

* 🥋 **Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (BJJ)** – Ground fighting, control, and submission techniques.
* 🛡️ **Traditional Ju-Jitsu (JJIF)** – A complete self-defense system divided into the following disciplines:
    * **Ne Waza** (ground techniques)
    * **Fighting** (striking techniques, throws, and ground fighting)

<br>

### Who is the training for?

We have structured groups for both **children** and **adults**, tailored to age and physical fitness level.
